Why is the 7-Second Gap Essential for Your Business?

Meta's anti-spam algorithms detect and flag accounts that send instant burst messages in under 1 second. Our engine enforces an automatic 7-second human pacing gap between automated sequence dispatches and simulates natural typing presence (composing...). This ensures your WhatsApp number retains a 🟢 High Quality Green Rating permanently.

How Spintax Protects Your Account

If you send 600 identical messages to 600 people, Meta's automated security monitors mark your phone as an automated broadcast bot. With Spintax, every single message has unique text phrasing:

// You write this in the campaign composer:

"{Hello|Hi|Greetings} {name}, we have {an exclusive|a special|a limited} offer on {service} in {city}!"

// The CRM sends different variations automatically:

• Contact 1 receives: "Hello Rahul, we have an exclusive offer on AC Repair in Chennai!"

• Contact 2 receives: "Hi Priya, we have a special offer on AC Repair in Bangalore!"

• Contact 3 receives: "Greetings Amit, we have a limited offer on AC Repair in Mumbai!"

MODULE 05

Dynamic Placeholders & Spintax Cheatsheet

Use these dynamic tags across Templates, Automations, Chatbot Flows, and Bulk Campaigns. The engine resolves real customer data automatically before sending.

{name} Customer Name

Replaced with contact's full name from WhatsApp or CRM clients/leads.

Output: "Vikram Sharma"
{company} Company Name

Replaced with business/shop name associated with this phone number.

Output: "Sharma Electronics"
{service} Inquired Service

Auto-extracted from Telecall inquiries or walk-in service category.

Output: "Solar Panel Installation"
{city} Customer City

Customer location extracted from client database or regional lookup.

Output: "Bangalore"
{amount} Invoice Amount

Formatted pending invoice or payment link amount in Rupees.

Output: "14,500.00"
{date} Current / Target Date

Current calendar date or appointment booking target date.

Output: "19 Aug 2026"
